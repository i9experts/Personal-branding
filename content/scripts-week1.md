## #1 — Tawakkul (Framework)
**HOOK:** "Tawakkul ka matlab haath pe haath rakh ke baithna NAHI hai."
**BODY:** "Ek sahabi ne Nabi ﷺ se poocha — main apna oont khula chhod doon aur Allah pe tawakkul karoon? Aap ﷺ ne farmaya: pehle oont baandho, phir tawakkul karo. Yahi framework hai: Step 1 — niyyat saaf karo. Step 2 — plan 100% banao, jaise sab kuch aap pe hai. Step 3 — result Allah pe chhod do, jaise kuch bhi aap pe nahi hai."
**CTA:** "Ye Framework number 1 hai. Number 2 kal — follow kar lo taake miss na ho."

## #2 — Pehli company ki galti (Builder)
**HOOK:** "Maine apni pehli company mein ye ek galti ki — aap mat karna."
**BODY:** "Main har kaam khud karta tha. Design bhi, sales bhi, accounts bhi. Result? Company meri speed se aage nahi badh saki. Business owner ka kaam har kaam karna nahi — sahi log dhoondhna hai. Pehle din se ek kaam delegate karo, chahe chhota sa ho."
**CTA:** "Aap kaunsa kaam abhi tak khud kar rahe ho? Comment mein batao."

## #3 — Paisa aur dunyadari (Myth-buster)
**HOOK:** "'Paisa kamana dunyadari hai' — ye soch aapko gareeb rakh rahi hai."
**BODY:** "Hazrat Abdur Rahman bin Auf, Hazrat Usman — ye sahaba bade traders the, aur Jannat ki basharat paane walon mein the. Masla paisa nahi, paisa dil mein ghus jaana hai. Halal rizq ki koshish khud ibadat hai, agar niyyat sahi ho."
**CTA:** "Kisi aise dost ko bhejo jo ye sunna chahiye."

## #4 — Subah ke 3 kaam (Framework)
**HOOK:** "Agar aap subah ye 3 kaam nahi karte, toh aapka din shuru hone se pehle hi haar chuka hai."
**BODY:** "Ek: Fajr ke baad 30 minute phone nahi. Do: din ka sirf EK sabse zaroori kaam likho. Teen: 5 minute mein din ka plan. Barakah subah mein hai — Nabi ﷺ ne dua ki: Ya Allah meri ummat ke liye subah mein barakat de."
**CTA:** "Kal subah try karo aur batao. Follow for Framework 5."

## #5 — Business se pehle 1 sawal (Builder)
**HOOK:** "Business shuru karne se pehle sirf ek sawal khud se poochho."
**BODY:** "Main kis ka masla hal kar raha hoon? Agar jawab clear nahi, toh product clear nahi hoga. EDUDEEN shuru karne se pehle humne 50 parents se baat ki. Product baad mein aaya, masla pehle samjha."
**CTA:** "Comment FRAMEWORK — main aapko free Life Design PDF DM karunga."
