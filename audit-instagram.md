### Pattern
- **Parenting/tarbiyat reels average ~1,070 views.** Business-design reels average ~550. Promos average ~430 with almost zero likes.
- **Shares drive reach.** Both big spikes on the graph (27 Oct, 7 Nov) are the only two reels with 5+ shares.
- **Faceless works.** Your top 3 reels are all faceless. Your face reel (925) is good for trust but not for reach.
- **Store promos hurt.** "7 din FREE", "solvexo.store" and "Price? Delivery?" teach the algorithm you're a shop, and they get almost no likes or shares.

## Who your audience actually is
- **56% women**, **72% aged 25–44**, **94% Pakistan**, **80% Karachi**.
- In plain words: **Karachi parents, mostly mothers, aged 25–44.** That's exactly who shared your School/Tuition reel.
- The 97.5% "English" figure is the language people's phones are set to, not the language they watch in. Keep Urdu/Roman-Urdu content.

## Best times to post (Pakistan time)
- **Friday 9:30 PM** (peak: Fri 9 PM–12 AM)
- **Tuesday & Thursday 7:30 PM** (6–9 PM)
- Other days: 8 PM. Avoid 3–9 AM.

## Decisions
1. **Main lane = "Tarbiyat aur Taleem"**: parenting + education for Muslim parents. About 70% of reels. Faceless, illustrated/animated, Urdu.
2. **Second lane = "Ghar aur Kaarobar ka Hisaab"**: money and small business, explained simply. About 30%. It's the format of your 1.5K "Profit" reel.
3. **Stop on this account:** Solvexo store promos, product posts, camera comparisons. Give Solvexo its own account.
4. **Turn winners into series:** "School ✅ Tuition ✅ — Part 2", "Profit ke bawajood — Part 2". Sequels to winners are the cheapest reach you'll ever get.
5. **Design every reel to be sent:** end with "Ye us maa/baap ko bhejein jinhe iski zaroorat hai." On screen, write something a mother would forward to her husband or sister.
6. **Post 1 Story daily:** a poll or question ("Aap ka bacha roz kitna screen time karta hai?"). Zero effort, and it builds the follower relationship.
7. **Fix the profile so the 3% who visit stay:** see the bio below. Pin School ✅, Profit, and اچھی صحبت.
