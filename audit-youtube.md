## Findings
1. **~41K of your 45.3K lifetime views are not on these 25 videos** (they total ≈4.1K). Something else — a deleted/private video, an old upload, or a collab — brought most of your audience. **Find it** (YouTube Studio → Content → sort by Views, include Private/Unlisted). That's your proven format.
2. **Uploads stopped.** Last long video 5 months ago, last Short 2 weeks ago — that's why it's 14 views/week.
3. **Three topics win, one loses:**
   - ✅ *Inner-life / emotional* ("successful but empty", "life is a system", "thak chuke ho") — 150–380
   - ✅ *Muslim history & identity* (Aurangzeb, Salahuddin) — 240–350, and these are naturally **faceless**
   - ✅ *Islamic reframes* (Tawakkul) — 217
   - ❌ *Corporate leadership/HR* (team motivation, appraisal, self-made man, expectations) — 49–90. **Stop.**
4. **Urdu/Roman-Urdu titles beat English titles** on similar topics (e.g. "Aap sust nahi ho" 155 vs "Why Hustle Culture" 77).
5. **Off-niche kills reach:** Kodak vs Fuji = 25 views, lowest on the channel.
6. **Titles are statements, not curiosity gaps.** Best performers ask a question that hits the viewer personally ("So why is life still confusing?").

## Direction (faceless-friendly)
Faceless format: your voiceover (or text-on-screen) + your design/collage style (the style of your 1.5K-view reel). Two series:
- **"Tareekh ka Framework"** — 45–60 sec Muslim-history stories with one life lesson (Salahuddin, Aurangzeb, Tariq bin Ziyad, Fatima al-Fihri…). Long version = 8–12 min documentary.
- **"Andar ki Baat"** — 20–30 sec emotional, 2nd-person Urdu lines over design visuals ("Aap thak chuke ho", "success ke baad emptiness"). Built to be *sent to a friend*. That's what "worth sharing" means: the viewer thinks "this is exactly him/her."
