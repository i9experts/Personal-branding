## 3. The "2 hours a week" system

**Your only job (Sunday, ~2 hrs):** sit in front of a camera and record **one 25–35 min podcast/solo episode** using the week's script in `content/30-day-calendar.csv`. Also record the **5 short hooks** listed for that week (each ~45 sec, read straight from the script). Upload the raw files to one Google Drive folder.

Everything after that is automated or delegated:

| Step | Tool (pick one) | Automation level |
|---|---|---|
| Long video → 10–15 vertical clips with captions | **Opus Clip** or **Vizard.ai** (auto-detects best moments, adds Urdu/English captions) | Fully automatic |
| Schedule clips to IG Reels + YouTube Shorts + Facebook at fixed times | **Metricool** (free tier) or **Buffer** — import `content/30-day-calendar.csv` | Fully automatic once queued |
| Full episode to YouTube | YouTube Studio schedule (Sunday 8 PM PKT) | Automatic |
| Comment "FRAMEWORK" → auto-DM a free PDF + follow request | **ManyChat** (Instagram) — this is the #1 legit follower multiplier | Fully automatic |
| Replies/engagement | 10 min/day replying to comments in the first hour after posting | Manual (cannot be safely automated) |

**Output per week from 2 hours of your time:** 1 long YouTube video + ~12 Reels/Shorts (≈2 per day on both platforms).

## 4. Growth levers that work at your size

1. **Collabs** — Instagram "Collab" posts with Muslim creators/educators at 10K–100K. Your followers count jumps with theirs. Aim for 2 per month. Your podcast is the perfect excuse: invite guests.
2. **Hook in first 2 seconds** — every script below starts with a pattern-interrupt line. Don't start with "Assalam o alaikum, aaj hum baat karenge…".
3. **Series format** — "Framework #1, #2, #3…" makes people follow to get the next one.
4. **ManyChat lead magnet** — "Comment FRAMEWORK and I'll send you the 7-day Life Design PDF." Comments boost reach and the DM asks them to follow.
5. **Pin your 3 best reels** on Instagram and make your best Short the channel trailer.
6. **Ramadan 2027 (starts ~8 Feb)** — your niche's biggest season. Plan a daily "30 Frameworks in 30 Days" series and start recording in January.
